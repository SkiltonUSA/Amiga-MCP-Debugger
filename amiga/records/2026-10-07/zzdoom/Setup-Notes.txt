ZZDoom 1.1 - A4000TX setup
Installed 7 October 2026 (Mac date).

Location: HDD50Gig:Games/ZZDoom

Double-click ZZDoom320 for 320x240, or ZZDoom640 for 640x480.
Both icons use WAD=DOOM1.WAD and a 65536-byte stack.

DOOM1.WAD is the original id Software Doom 1.9 shareware episode,
obtained from the idgames mirror at ftp.fu-berlin.de.
Original shareware instructions are in SharewareDocs.

Music is disabled with the NOMUSIC tooltype because the Roland MT-32
is not connected. AHI sound effects remain enabled. This does not
change the global AHI or CAMD configuration.

Shell launch:
  CD HDD50Gig:Games/ZZDoom
  Stack 65536
  ZZDoom320 DOOM1.WAD -nomusic

To enable external MIDI later, remove NOMUSIC from the icon and set
CAMDPORT=out.0 for the configured native serial MIDI route. Doom's
General MIDI music is not an MT-32-native score; instrument mapping
may need attention. No MIDI notes or instrument tests were sent here.

Quit using Doom's menu (Escape, Quit Game, confirm Y). Do not launch
ZZQuake or another ARM Core1 application at the same time.

Existing XX19c/XACP 1.7 firmware and matching ZZ9000.card retained.
No firmware, global system driver, or startup-script change was needed.
