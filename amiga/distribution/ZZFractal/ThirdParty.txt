ZZFractal third-party build/runtime notices

The application and its embedded ARM worker were built specifically for this
project. No ZZ9000 firmware image, Amiga ROM, Workbench, P96 driver or commercial
game data is redistributed in this archive.

The Amiga executable is linked using the libnix standard C runtime and the
Amiga library stubs from the pinned amigadev/crosstools toolchain. It does
not require an external ixemul.library or libnix4.library.
